FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to be physically present or share VPN access?

No. Public-facing targets are tested entirely remotely. For internal or staging targets, we agree on access (VPN, allow-listed IP, or test accounts) during scoping.

How do you price an engagement?

Starting rates are listed above for a standard scope. Larger apps, more roles, or more endpoints change the quote. You always get a fixed price before any work starts.

Will testing break my production system?

No destructive testing. I respect rate limits and agreed windows, use minimal proofs of concept, and stop at the first value that proves impact.

How is severity decided?

By the impact I can actually demonstrate, scored with CVSS. I don't inflate findings or pad reports with scanner noise.

Is retesting included?

Yes. Once your team ships fixes, I re-verify each finding and confirm closure at no extra cost.

Can you sign an NDA?

Yes, before any details are exchanged. I'm KYC-verified on Com Olho and can work under your NDA or mine.

Do you invoice in INR?

I can invoice in INR for Indian companies and in USD for international clients. Share your billing requirements when you request a quote.
